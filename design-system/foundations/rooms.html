Rooms

Law 6, restated. The platform is not one ground, nor two — it is a building with rooms, and every screen declares which room it stands in. A room supplies three things: the wall, the light, and the floor-level shadow. Panels are the furniture placed in it, and they work in any room. What a room does not decide

A room sets the wall and the light. It never sets status — that stays the four-rung ladder plus a glyph and an uppercase word, in any room (Laws 2 and 3). It never sets proportion — type still climbs by √φ and spacing is still Fibonacci, in any room (Law 8). And it never sets refusal — a declined action is stamped in ink on the page whether that page is hanging on brick or cork (Law 7).
