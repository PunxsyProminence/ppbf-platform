PPBF Design System · Foundations

Palette

Three ramps build the platform: hide for ground, brass for chassis, bone for type. None of them is allowed to mean anything. Meaning is carried entirely by a four-rung status ladder, which is the only saturated colour in the system.

Law 1 — Brass is the chassis, never the message. Frames, rivets, rope, bezels, tile edges, button faces. Brass is what the platform is built from. The moment gold starts meaning something, every frame on the page begins lying — so it never carries state, and no rung of the ladder sits near it.

Law 2 — Saturated colour means safety or status. Nothing else may use it. Against leather and brass, a saturated pixel is unmissable. That is the entire budget, and it is spent on the Gate Matrix. No saturated links, no green success toasts, no red for emphasis.

Law 3 — the greyscale test

As designed

✓Cleared ◉Monitor ▲Restricted ✕Locked

Four hues, four glyphs, four words.

Colour removed

✓Cleared ◉Monitor ▲Restricted ✕Locked

Still fully readable. This is the state a board packet prints in, and the state a colour-blind coach reads on the floor — so it is the state the ladder is designed for.
