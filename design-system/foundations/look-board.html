PPBF Design System · Foundations

Look Board

One page that fixes the merged style before any screen changes, so every room follows it instead of each room deciding again. Owner decision OD-2026-09-28-012: the two looks merge — the real gym is drawn in the early gritty style, for screens and for images — and the board is built around The Floor, with The Floor's own background and one Floor panel as its samples ("kinda a cross between a and c").

Nothing here is invented. The early style is read from Jason's Canva folder PPBF LOOKS AND FEELS (opened 2026-09-29); the building is read from docs/REAL-GYM-REFERENCE-LOCK.md §2, which is itself amended from his ten photographs. Where the two disagree, it says so rather than picking.

The merge, as two sources

What the early set brings

  • Aged tan canvas as the paper — olive-tan, stained, never white.
  • Dark charcoal iron as the chassis: header band, footers, frames.
  • Gold foil as the rule and the emblem — a hairline, not a fill.
  • Oxidised red as stain and as status, bled into the ground.
  • Stencil caps as the voice, with ADAPT AND OVERCOME a fixture.
  • Dense grids of small bordered cards — an operations board, not a column.
Canva · PPBF LOOKS AND FEELS · 22 items · opened 2026-09-29. Jason, 2026-09-28: "the logos and things will need to be updated thos were kinda mock ups".

What the building brings

  • A painted, zoned floor — red at the ring, blue with a taped grid, grey with white markings, green carpet at the lockers.
  • Honey reclaimed plank over most of the walls.
  • Blackboard paint — the wall itself is written on, in chalk.
  • Homemade structure: timber post-and-beam, black steel scaffold pipe.
  • The ring: teal canvas, a faded brewery roundel, red and blue posts, white pads.
  • Flat fluorescent light, uneven and slightly green. Never cinematic.
docs/REAL-GYM-REFERENCE-LOCK.md §2, amended 2026-09-26 from ten owner photographs.

The rule this board sets. The gym supplies the surfaces — plank, blackboard, painted floor zones, steel pipe, teal canvas. The early set supplies the treatment — aged tan panels on dark iron, a gold hairline, an oxidised stain, stencil caps, and content laid out as a dense board of real objects. A room is built by choosing which of the gym's surfaces it stands on; it is not built by choosing a new style.

The ground — The Floor

Drawn in CSS, no image asset — the same rule foundations/materials.html works under, so a floor kiosk renders it offline. It also carries no lettering at all, which is how the plates' zero-lettering rule gets satisfied structurally instead of by inspection after the fact: eight generated plates were rejected for lettering, two of them for "EVERLAST" on a bag. The one exception the owner has granted — the ring canvas keeps its IRON CITY roundel (OD-2026-09-28-013) — is drawn here as the shape without the words, because at this scale the words would be illegible anyway. The four zone colours are proposed, not measured: they are read off the photographs by eye and need Jason's on the tablet before they become tokens.

Colour, and what is still reserved

Law 2 stands, with one change. Saturated colour means safety or status — except red, which is the club's colour and is no longer reserved (OD-2026-09-29-001). --locked (#A81E22) still means a medical stop and nothing else may wear it.

Law 3 stands. Colour is never the only channel. Every status carries a glyph and an uppercase label, as the stamp above does. This is what lets the gritty ground go dark without the safety signals going with it.

Laws 1, 4, 6 and 8 are retired (OD-2026-09-28-009). Brass is no longer the chassis — dark iron is, and brass is the hairline. The materials list above replaces law 6's five.

Named through tokens only. The retired faces — Alfa Slab One, Oswald, Special Elite, Caveat, UnifrakturCook — are never spelled out in source; legacyVisualVocabulary.test.ts holds that at zero. The tokens themselves still resolve to those faces through the legacy sheet, which is what OD-2026-09-28-009 item 4 means by correcting the contract "to what is built". Replacing the faces is a separate decision and this board does not make it.

What this board does not decide

  • The four floor-zone colours. Read off photographs by eye. They need Jason's eye on a gym tablet before they become tokens.
  • The typefaces. The early set is military stencil; the live tokens still point at the retired faces. Whether the merged look keeps them, and which face carries ADAPT AND OVERCOME, is open.
  • The emblem. The early set's laurel-and-heart logos are mock-ups by the owner's own account and are deliberately not drawn here.
  • Whether any room still wants a photograph. This board shows the ground can be material rather than a plate. The plate mechanism is untouched and still works.
  • The other sixteen rooms. docs/ROOM-MAP.md is the build order. Each room picks its surfaces from the materials above; it does not pick a new style.
