1 · The display voice — Alfa Slab One

The old law said stencil. None of the reference art is stencil — stencil letterforms carry bridges through the strokes, and not one reference header has them. The art is a jobbing printshop: heavy wood-type slab, letterpress-printed, ink broken up at the edges. Three candidates were compared here; Alfa Slab One won and is now --font-display. A slab beat the grotesque because every reference header has visible serif feet, and beat the condensed face because Oswald is already --font-ui — a display voice that is the body voice at a heavier weight is not a second voice at all.

3 · Light — and shadows that obey it

4 · Placement — rotation and overlap, merged

These were two notes on my list and they are one fix. Tilting every card but keeping tidy lanes looks like a tilted grid; overlapping while axis-aligned looks like a z-index bug. Rotation is deterministic (nth-child, not random) so a screen renders identically every time — paper that wanders between reloads reads as breakage, not craft.
