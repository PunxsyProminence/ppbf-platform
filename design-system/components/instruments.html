Instruments

The things a coach reads and touches. Metrics are brass instruments rather than number cards, because a needle communicates where in the range a value sits before you have read the digits — which is the whole job of a metrics bar.

Status badges

✓Cleared ◉Monitor ▲Restricted ✕Locked

The Layer 11 ladder. Each badge mixes its hue 26% into the hide for the fill and 62% for the border, so it sits in the leather rather than on top of it, and stays legible against every panel in the system.

Controls

One brass button per view (Law 2) — everything secondary is a ghost outline. Brass buttons carry a lit top edge and a dark lower lip so they read as cast metal you press. .btn--kiosk is 55px tall with 19.1px type for the gym floor (Law 5). Destructive actions use --locked, the same red as the safety lock: both mean halt.

Fields

Inputs are recessed into the leather — a dark well with an inset shadow and a thin brass rim. They read as cut into the panel, which is why they never need a label floating inside them to look like inputs.
