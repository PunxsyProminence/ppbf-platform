PPBF Design System · Components

Surfaces

The objects coaches read. A chalkboard for today (erasable), cork for announcements (permanent), leather tabs for navigation (objects you pick), and a brass-ruled governance banner (a standing condition, never a warning).

Chalkboard schedule

Today's Sessions
08:00 Fundamentals Kustin Ring 1
09:30 Conditioning Team B Ring 1
11:30 Advanced Jack C. Ring 2
13:00 Recovery Evey Ring 3

Everything on the board is .chalk or .chalk-dim. The divider under the heading is a brass rule. Erase the board at end of day — it belongs to today only. When a session ends, the chalk can go with it, which is the whole point of a chalkboard.

Cork board & announcements

Tournament prep starts Monday

New equipment arriving Thursday

Board meeting postponed to next week

Gym closed Sunday for maintenance

Cork is woven and multi-tonal, generated from layered radial gradients and a finer grain. Paper notes are torn at the bottom via clip-path (22 points, no image asset). Pins are brass at 34° light with a red head for visual pop. Notes rotate ±2° so the board never looks gridded. Announcements are permanent, filed paper — unlike the chalkboard, they accumulate.

The pin is optional. Use .pin--brass for neutral announcements (a routine schedule change) and the red default for urgent ones (closure, incident, redirect).

Leather tag navigation

Athlete Floor Coach Desk Grant Reports Ledger Settings

Luggage-tag style with a brass grommet and ring at the left. Each tag is --tap tall (55px) — touchable on the floor. The dashed inset border is stitching. On hover or current page, the tag lightens and gets a ring of brass around it. The staggered left margin (done per-item with :nth-child in the demo) suggests they're hung from a rail, not sitting in a grid. No two tags need the same margin — randomness within a range looks natural.

Governance banner

Layer 0 Governance
All actions are logged and attributable. Coach decisions are reversible only by the head coach. Jason final approval required for production changes.

The governance banner is a standing condition on every staff screen (Law 7). It is a neutral brass strip with a transparent background — it is permanent, not an alert, so it wears neither warning nor success colour. The message is always the same: Layer 0 is watching, decisions are recorded, and approval gates exist. If this banner ever turns red or orange, it stops meaning "governance" and starts meaning "error", which breaks the promise. So it stays brass and bone forever.

How surfaces compose

Chalkboard is data that changes every day — erase it at the end of shift. Cork is data that accumulates — file it, pin it, keep it until someone takes it down. Tags are structure — the permanent topology of the app. Governance banner is the standing promise — it does not move, it does not change colour, and a coach can read it without looking up.

This is why they are not the same material. A participant running on the floor should see a chalkboard and know: "this is today". A guardian in the office should see cork and announcements and know: "this is official notice". A coach should see tags and banner and know: "I am inside governance".
