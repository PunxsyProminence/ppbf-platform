- Difficulty: advanced
- Category: tactical

**Purpose** (INFERRED)

> Trains the compound feint live: one feint draws a reaction, a second draws the counter-reaction, then the real shot, scored on whether the second reaction was drawn.

- Note: adapted: the record's feint mechanism and drill line.

**Target behaviour** (INFERRED)

- Sources:
  - DOC-027 (Punxsy Master Source Manual v1, Feint Standard): "A feint should lead into a real choice: score, reposition, reset, or deny."
  - DOC-027 (Punxsy Master Source Manual v1, False Open and Trigger Creation Standard): "Require athletes to reset if the reaction is not there."
- Note: adapted: DOC-027's feint and false-open lines with the record's mechanism.

**Setup** (INFERRED)

- Note: adapted: the record's drill line; my addition: the count and where the coach stands.

**What good looks like** (INFERRED)

> The first feint draws a readable reaction; the second draws the counter-reaction.
