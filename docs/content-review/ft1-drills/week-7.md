## 7.1 Monday, footwork: Faster footwork with position check using accelerated but verified movements

**DOC-123 cell:** "Faster footwork with position check using accelerated but verified movements" (Week 7 table, Monday, Footwork).

**Setup** (INFERRED)

> Open floor with a straight tape line per athlete and room to step forward, back and to both sides. Athletes start in stance on the line; the coach stands side-on to the lines, where feet, hips and guard can all be seen.

- Sources:
  - DOC-072 Drill_Registry S01-004 (Line Pressure Base): "floor line/ring rope marker"
  - LR-046 (The Long Road, Part 3, 1.3 Technical priorities, Months 0-3): "Use lines on the floor, a ladder, cones, or a partner mirroring."
  - DOC-027 (Punxsy Master Source Manual v1, Coach Positioning Standard): "Lead coach stands where the entry, main error point, and finish of the rep can be seen."
- Note: adapted: the DOC-072 floor-line equipment (S01-004, a stance row) first, the Long Road floor-line tool and the DOC-027 coach position; my addition: room to step in every direction.

**Steps** (INFERRED)

> Coach gives the cue "Get set first." and the athlete sets stance on the line.
>
> Athlete steps a short footwork pattern at normal pace and freezes in stance on the coach's call; the coach checks the freeze.
>
> Athlete repeats the same pattern a little faster each time, freezing in stance at the end; speed goes up only while every freeze is clean.
>
> When position is lost for speed, the coach gives one brief cue and the athlete drops back to the last pace that held.
>
> Athlete keeps working for the time the session plan sets.

- Sources:
  - DOC-123: "Monday introduces increased speed within controlled parameters."

  - DOC-123: "Sacrifice position for speed"
  - DOC-123: "using brief verbal cues"
- Note: adapted: the DOC-072 freeze check is the 'position check' in the cell name; the DOC-027 rhythm lines set the pace steps; my addition: repeat the same pattern faster each time. No time or rep count is given.

**What good looks like** (INFERRED)

> Every freeze lands in stance: feet not crossed, base not narrowed, guard up, eyes forward.
> Speed rises only as far as the freeze stays clean.
> The head stays level while the feet move, with no bouncing off both feet at once.

- Sources:
  - DOC-072 Drill_Registry S01-001 (Mirror Base Freeze): "base, guard, eyes, and feet are usable at each freeze without crossing, squaring, or delayed reset"
  - DOC-027 (Punxsy Master Source Manual v1, Decision Speed vs Decision Quality): "The system values correct speed, not reckless speed."
