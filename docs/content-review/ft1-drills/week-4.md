**DOC-123 cell:** "Punch delivery while preserving stance structure" (Week 4 table, Monday, Footwork).

**Already in the drill, kept as written:** what good looks like.

**Setup** (INFERRED)

> Open floor, one straight tape line per athlete and a short line at 45 degrees for the rear foot; athletes stand across the line in stance, facing a mirror or the coach, and punch in shadow. The coach stands side-on, where feet, hips and head can be seen as the punch goes out.

- Sources:
  - LR-046 (The Long Road, Part 3, 1.3 Technical priorities, Months 0-3): "feet too narrow (draw a line on the floor and stand across it); square to the opponent (drill from a taped 45° line)"
  - DOC-072 Drill_Registry S01-001 (Mirror Base Freeze): "mirror or coach"
  - DOC-072 Drill_Registry S01-002 (Punch-Freeze-Guard): "bag/pads/shadow"
- Note: adapted: the Week 1 stance setup (Long Road floor-line fixes, unverified, RULES s4) with DOC-072's shadow option; my addition: where the coach stands.

**Steps** (INFERRED)

> Athlete sets stance across the line. Cue: "Get set first."
>
> Athlete delivers one punch the coach names, in shadow, bringing the hand back faster than it went out.
>
> Athlete freezes after the return; the coach checks that the feet are still on their lines, the weight is near 50/50 and the guard is back.
>
> Coach gives one short cue to fix one thing, and the athlete punches again from the set.

- Sources:
  - DOC-123: "Monday introduces punch delivery while preserving stance structure."

  - LR-046 (The Long Road, Part 3, 1.3 Technical priorities, Months 0-3): "Retraction faster than extension"

  - DOC-123: "using brief verbal cues rather than extended explanations during active drilling"
- Note: adapted: DOC-123's Monday line with DOC-072 Punch-Freeze-Guard's freeze and pass standard, and Long Road jab and stance lines (unverified, RULES s4); my addition: the coach naming the punch. 'Retraction faster than extension' is the Long Road's jab line, applied here to any named punch.
