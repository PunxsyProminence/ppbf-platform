**DOC-123 cell:** "Planned footwork sequences with defined start-middle-end structure" (Week 6 table, Monday, Footwork).

**Setup** (INFERRED)

> Open floor with a tape mark or cone for a start spot and an end spot per athlete. The coach stands where every athlete's start and end can be seen.

>
> Athlete sets stance on the start mark with the cue "Get set first."
>
> Athlete moves through the middle steps in order at a controlled pace, staying in stance.
>
> Athlete arrives on the end mark and freezes in stance to show the sequence is finished.

## 6.2 Monday, head movement: Defense-to-offense transitions with clear phase separation

`drl_7a4ff70fc0fe8c`. Contact: none (unchanged). Cue(s): Get set first.

**DOC-123 cell:** "Defense-to-offense transitions with clear phase separation" (Week 6 table, Monday, Head Movement).

**Already in the drill, kept as written:** what good looks like.

**Setup** (INFERRED)

> Pairs face each other at a distance where neither athlete's punches reach; the feeder's attacks and the athlete's counters stop short. The coach stands side-on to see each phase.

- Sources:
  - DOC-123: "Defense-to-offense transitions with clear phase separation"
- Note: adapted: the DOC-123 cell; my addition: out-of-reach distance (contact level none), where the coach stands.

**Steps** (INFERRED)

> Athlete sets stance with the cue "Get set first."
>
> Defense phase: the feeder throws one stop-short punch and the athlete makes one defense with the head.
>
> Recovery phase: the athlete visibly returns to stance, breath and eyes; a short pause marks the line between phases.
>
> Offense phase: the athlete throws a planned counter that stops short, or resets if the picture is closed.
>
> Athlete ends set, and the coach checks that each phase was clear before the next rep.

- Sources:
  - DOC-123: "Get set first."
  - DOC-027 (Punxsy Master Source Manual v1, Post Defensive Recovery Standard): "Separate defense from countering until the athlete can recover shape first."
  - DOC-027 (Punxsy Master Source Manual v1, Post Defensive Recovery Standard): "Build drills where the defense must end in a visible reset or exit."
  - DOC-027 (Punxsy Master Source Manual v1, Defense to Offense Transfer Standard): "Use if-then rules after defense such as see first, then score or leave."
  - DOC-123: "Monday introduces planned action sequences with clear beginning and end points."
- Note: adapted: the DOC-027 recovery correction ('separate defense from countering') is the phase separation; my addition: naming the three phases and the pause.

**Scale levels** (B is the starting point)

- **A** (INFERRED): The defense is called in advance, and a freeze is held after it until the coach says go; then the counter.
  - Constraint: Pre-called defense; freeze until go.
  - Coach watches: Is the freeze set before the counter starts.
  - Source: DOC-072 Drill_Registry S06-001 (Single Straight Defend Return): "pre-called defense"
  - Note: adapted: the DOC-072 regression; my addition: the freeze until the coach's go.
- **B, start** (INFERRED): The drill as designed: defense, visible recovery, then offense or reset, each phase clearly separated.
  - Coach watches: Can the coach see where one phase ends and the next begins.
  - Source: DOC-123: "Defense-to-offense transitions with clear phase separation"
  - Source: DOC-027 (Punxsy Master Source Manual v1, Post Defensive Recovery Standard): "Separate defense from countering until the athlete can recover shape first."
  - Note: adapted: the DOC-123 cell and DOC-027 correction.
- **C** (INFERRED): The pause between phases gets shorter and the feeder varies side and timing; each phase must still be visible.
  - Constraint: Shorter pause; random side and timing.
  - Coach watches: Do the phases blur as the pause shrinks.
  - Source: DOC-072 Drill_Registry S06-001 (Single Straight Defend Return): "random side/tempo straight feed"
  - Note: adapted: the DOC-072 progression (random side and tempo); my addition: the pause between phases getting shorter. Feeds still stop short.

**Stop rules** (defaults a coach can override)

1. Stop the rep when defense and counter blur into one rushed action with no recovery between. *(kind: intent_drift; INFERRED; tier CONVENTION)*

   - Source: DOC-072 Drill_Registry S06-001 (Single Straight Defend Return): "dead shell, counter from broken base, over-slip"
