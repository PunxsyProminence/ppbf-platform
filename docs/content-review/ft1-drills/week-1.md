**DOC-123, this week:**

> Week 1: Hold Position - Foundation Building
> Week 1 establishes the foundational principle of maintaining stable positioning throughout all movements. Monday introduces core stance mechanics and static holds. Tuesday reinforces positioning through repetition drills. Wednesday applies positional control under coach-called combinations. Thursday tests position maintenance under sustained effort and fatigue. Optional punch introduction for Week 1: straight punches in shadow boxing only, available if earned through demonstrated positional control. Punches must be removed if student loses stance integrity, rushes movements, or stops responding to coaching cues.
