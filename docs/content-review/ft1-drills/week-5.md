## 5.1 Monday, footwork: Retreat footwork without panic steps using measured backward movement

`drl_c03f5a5387fef1`. Contact: none (unchanged). Cue(s): Get set first.

**DOC-123 cell:** "Retreat footwork without panic steps using measured backward movement" (Week 5 table, Monday, Footwork).

**Already in the drill, kept as written:** what good looks like.

**Setup** (INFERRED)

> One straight floor line per pair, with room behind the athlete to step back along it. The athlete stands on the line in stance; the partner faces them at a distance the coach sets and walks forward along the line to give the pressure, never touching. The coach stands side-on to the line to see feet and hips.

- Sources:
  - DOC-072 Drill_Registry S01-004 (Line Pressure Base): "non-contact pressure along line"
  - DOC-072 Drill_Registry S01-004 (Line Pressure Base): "floor line/ring rope marker"
  - LR-046 (The Long Road, Part 3, 1.3 Technical priorities, Months 0-3): "Use lines on the floor, a ladder, cones, or a partner mirroring."
- Note: adapted: the DOC-072 row's constraint and equipment become the setup; my addition: partner distance set by the coach, where the coach stands.

**Steps** (INFERRED)

> Coach gives the cue "Get set first." and the athlete sets stance on the line.
>
> Partner steps forward slowly; the athlete answers each partner step with one measured step back, rear foot first and lead foot following, feet never crossing.
>
> Athlete breathes out with each step and keeps eyes on the partner, not the floor.
>
> Partner stops; the athlete stops in stance and holds it until the coach sees it set, then the partner walks forward again.
>
> Coach gives one short cue between passes to fix one thing.

- Sources:
  - DOC-123: "Get set first."
  - DOC-072 Drill_Registry S01-004 (Line Pressure Base): "Maintain stance while being moved backward or laterally"
  - LR-046 (The Long Road, Part 3, 1.3 Technical priorities, Months 0-3): "rear foot first going back, never crossing"
  - DOC-027 (Punxsy Master Source Manual v1, Breathing and Composure Standard): "Teach exhale during action and controlled inhale during recovery."

- Note: adapted: the DOC-072 row is the drill picture, LR-046 gives the step order, DOC-027 the exhale with action (Monday's breathing line); my addition: one step per partner step, the stop-and-hold between steps.

**DOC-123 cell:** "Relaxed head movement under slow feed with partner progression" (Week 5 table, Monday, Head Movement).

**Setup** (INFERRED)

> Pairs face each other at a distance where the feeder's straight punch stops short of the athlete's face; feeds are slow and never touch. The coach stands to the side where both heads and both stances can be seen.

- Sources:
  - DOC-123: "Relaxed head movement under slow feed with partner progression"
  - DOC-027 (Punxsy Master Source Manual v1, Feeder and Receiver Role Standard): "The feeder is responsible for giving the correct picture consistently enough that the receiver can learn from it."

**Steps** (INFERRED)

> Coach states the feeder's job before the drill: slow, stop-short feeds, one at a time, no trying to win the drill.
>
> Athlete sets stance with the cue "Get set first." and breathes out slowly before the first feed.
>
> Feeder throws one slow straight that stops short; the athlete moves the head just off the line of the punch and returns to centre in stance.
>
> Athlete keeps shoulders and jaw loose and breathes out with each movement; the feeder waits until the athlete is set again before the next feed.
>
> Coach raises the feed (pace, or the side it comes from) only while the athlete stays relaxed; this is the partner progression.

**What good looks like** (UNVERIFIED)

> The head moves only as far as needed to clear the line, then returns to centre.
> Shoulders stay down and breathing keeps going through each feed.
> Stance and guard are the same after the movement as before it.
> The athlete waits for the feed instead of moving early.

- Sources:
  - no source passage
- Note: no source passage; drafted from the drill's cell and the Week 5 Monday line (relaxed defensive postures, controlled breathing). My addition: the four checks (no passage names this drill's skill).

  - Note: adapted: the DOC-123 cell is the drill as designed.

## 5.4 Monday, punch integration: See-decide-counter without rushing

**Already in the drill, kept as written:** what good looks like.

**Setup** (INFERRED)

> Pairs face each other at a distance where neither athlete's punches reach; the feeder throws slow single straights that stop short, and the athlete's counter also stops short. The coach stands side-on to both.

- Sources:
  - DOC-123: "See-decide-counter without rushing"
  - DOC-072 Drill_Registry S06-001 (Single Straight Defend Return): "single straight feed; one defense; one return or reset"
- Note: adapted: the DOC-072 row's single-feed constraint; my addition: the out-of-reach distance (contact level none), where the coach stands.

**Steps** (INFERRED)

> Coach gives the cue "Get set first."; the athlete sets stance and breathes out.
>
> Feeder throws one slow straight that stops short; the athlete makes one small defense (head off the line, or a step).
>
> Athlete recovers stance, breath and eyes, sees what is there, then decides.
>
> If the picture is open, the athlete throws one counter that stops short; if not, the athlete resets or steps to a better spot.
>
> Coach praises a calm reset as much as a clean counter.

- Sources:
  - DOC-123: "Get set first."

  - DOC-027 (Punxsy Master Source Manual v1, Defense to Offense Transfer Standard): "Reinforce good non-counter defensive outcomes when they preserve the system."
- Note: adapted: DOC-027 recovery and transfer standards and the DOC-072 cue give the order; my addition: how the open picture is shown is left to the coach.

  - Coach watches: Is each choice made after the recovery, or before it.
  - Source: DOC-123: "See-decide-counter without rushing"
  - Source: DOC-072 Drill_Registry S06-002 (Defend Or Exit): "athlete reads open/closed counter window"
  - Note: adapted: the DOC-123 cell; the DOC-072 read is the designed decision.
- **C** (INFERRED): The feeder varies the side and timing of the slow feeds and how long the picture stays open; the choice is still counter or reset.
  - Constraint: Random side and timing.
  - Coach watches: Does a delayed or switched feed make the athlete guess.
  - Source: DOC-072 Drill_Registry S06-001 (Single Straight Defend Return): "random side/tempo straight feed"
