> Week 2 advances to controlled movement with clean stops, emphasizing deceleration and balance recovery. Monday introduces directional stepping with deliberate stops. - Sources:

**Steps** (INFERRED)

> Coach states the observer's job in one line (watch head, feet and guard, name one thing) and gives the cue "Get set first."
>
> Athlete slips to one side, returns to centre, and freezes in stance with the guard up.
>
> Observer checks the freeze and names one thing; any touch is a light glove touch at the point the coach sets.
>
> Athlete repeats to the other side; after the time the session plan sets, roles switch.
