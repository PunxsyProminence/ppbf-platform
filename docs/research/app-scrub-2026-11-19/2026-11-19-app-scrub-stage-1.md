- The app has 165 pages. Beyond each role's home, most are reached through one header menu ("Rooms") of up to 114 entries, sorted by rooms of a building rather than by job (OBSERVED).

**Q1 — Who sets an athlete's medical clearance (CS-01)**
Official form: "The Clearance Board tells coaches 'Clearance records are set by the office' (coach/sports-medicine/page.tsx:729, 810), but the Decision Loop's 'Medical Administrative Status' lets any coach with access set Pending, Cleared, Restricted or Not Cleared (coach/decision-loop/page.tsx:1404-1474). The API admits coaches on purpose, and its comment marks the question as yours (api/pilot/shadow/medical-status/route.ts:109-114). **A: Only the gym office (admin) sets clearance; the coach control is removed, and the Clearance Board's words stay true (Recommended).** B: Coaches and the office both set it, on the Clearance Board, and the words say so. C: Leave both screens as they are."
Plain English: one screen tells a coach the office sets clearance, and another screen lets the coach set it. You decide who may set it; the app should then say the same thing everywhere.

**Q2 — Forgotten PIN wording (OFF-01)**
Official form: "The sign-in help says a gym admin *or coach* 'can create a new account or reset your PIN' (components/SignInPanel.tsx:829-832). The reset API admits organization admins only (api/pilot/admin/accounts/pin-reset/route.ts:28), and no coach screen has the control. **A: Change the words to 'ask the gym office' (Recommended).** B: Give coaches a PIN reset control (a new permission). C: Leave as is."
Plain English: a child at the kiosk is told a coach can fix their PIN, but no coach can.

**Q3 — Parent hub safety line (FAM-01)**

C: Leave as is."
Plain English: a parent glancing at the hub can read 'nothing to report' while their child has a medical restriction. Note: your rule against new warnings (OD-2026-11-10-001 RULES s1) may apply to A. B adds no warning.

**Q4 — Which mark a critical safety escalation wears (CS-10, CF-19)**
